Swimming with Turtles

Five reading levels: Pre-A, Pre-AA, A, AA and AAA. Four chapters follow Noor, Maya, Theo, Ben and guide Jo. A final sea-turtle labelling diagram completes the assessment.
Pre-AA: developing-reader text; 2 story blanks, 1 question and 2 matching pairs per chapter; 3 turtle labels; 23 marks altogether.

Each chapter requires all story blanks, questions and matching pairs to be filled. Every question tab and Matching must be opened before the chapter can lock. Valid answers may be incorrect; placement feedback does not reveal marks. Locked chapters stay locked.

Use A− and A+ for text size. Drag choices onto blue landing pads and release when the pad glows, or select a choice and then select a pad. A green flash confirms placement. Placed answers can be moved or replaced before locking.

Teacher code: 259. Voice starts disabled. The teacher can enable voice and review or export results with the same controls as Mountain Biking. Progress remains on the device; export results before resetting.

Print Parent-Word-Cards.pdf at Actual Size / 100%. Five sheets contain 40 words, eight per sheet, with cutting guides and no story summary. A family can practise one sheet per day: read the word, explain it, and use it in an original sentence.

Cloze answers save after every placement and remain saved when switching views, moving through text parts, continuing to a new chapter, and resuming the same Camper ID and reading level on the same device.
